Description

4C: Custom Clips Cleverly Cut is a puzzle game in which you need to solve puzzles by manipulating objects like clips in a cutting software. Finishing levels grants you money to unlock new levels and customization options for your simulated pc.

Story

In 4C you are a newcomer in the video cutting scene and registered on the 4C platform to cut peoples videos with your cutting software 4C Studio 2005 to earn cash and climb the cutter rankings. Each puzzle is a task from one of your many diverse clients. Each client has their own personality and reasons for wanting to have their clips cut and uploaded on the internet. While progressing the game you slowly build a friendship with each of them.

Gameplay and Features

In 4C you need to solve puzzles by manipulating objects like clips in a cutting software. Place objects, adjust their position, and perfect their timing to give your clients the effects they wish. The shorter your solution is, the more stars and reward money you receive from your diverse cast of clients. Use your earned money to buy expansion packs for your cutting software, which include new objects or effects and makes you available for new tasks, or customization options and apps.

Improvements

The main thing I would like to add are more levels. While there are 16 levels, most of them are quite simple. The reason for that is that I wanted to showcase all the different ways these mechanics could be used before making levels more complex. This would of course also mean further character development with the existing clients, as well as adding new ones. I would also like to make the design a but more true to the time and add more nostalgia and references to the early internet viral video time I wanted to reference with the setting.
